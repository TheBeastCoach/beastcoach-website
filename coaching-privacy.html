Coaching privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026.

What we collect

  • Your onboarding answers: about you, your goal, your food and training, your health check and your weigh-in day.
  • Your WhatsApp number and time zone, so we can reach you and work out your days.
  • What you log: meals, steps, workouts and weigh-ins.
  • Your payment and plan details, and whether you agreed to news and offers.

Who can see it

  • Coaching plans (Essential, Core and The Beast): Stefan can see your onboarding answers and your logs, so he can build and adjust your plan.
  • Basic: nobody at Beast Coach can see your meals, steps, workouts or weigh-ins. You see only your plan, status and billing.

AI providers

To work out nutrition, the meal text and meal photos you send to Beast AI are sent to an AI provider. The provider processes them to return an estimate. Do not send anything you would not want processed this way.

Export or delete your data

You can ask for a copy of your data, or for your data to be deleted, by emailing info@beastcoach.fit. Deleting your data removes your onboarding answers, plans, logs and notes.

What we keep after deleting your data

We keep a customer record after your data is deleted. It holds your name, email, what you bought and when, and your email choices. We keep it for tax records and to respect your email choices, so that we do not email you after you have unsubscribed.
