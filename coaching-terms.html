Coaching terms

Last updated 25 November 2026. All prices are in Australian dollars (AUD).

What each plan includes

PlanPrice and billingWhat you get
Basic$2.50 a week, billed as $30 every 12 weeksYou run it yourself. Beast AI meal logging, daily steps, your weekly weigh-in and daily targets. No coach, no plans, no calls.
Essential$110 for 12 weeks, paid up frontOne personalised meal and training plan for 12 weeks, with one kick-off video call on WhatsApp at the start.
Core$299 for 12 weeks, paid up frontEverything in Essential, plus your logged meals reviewed each week, a weekly WhatsApp check-in, two extra video calls at weeks 4 and 8, plans updated at weeks 4 and 8, form checks on WhatsApp, and The Discipline Path.
The Beast$699 for 12 weeks, paid up frontEverything in Core, plus a weekly 1:1 WhatsApp video call, direct WhatsApp access, live technique sessions on video, and plan changes whenever you need them.

The Discipline Path is included with Core and The Beast. It is normally $37.

Paying

  • Coaching plans (Essential, Core and The Beast) are paid up front for 12 weeks.
  • Basic is billed as $30 every 12 weeks.
  • Beast AI gives estimates, not lab measurements.

Refunds

  • You get a full refund if you ask before your plan starts.
  • Once your plan has been delivered, we do not give refunds for it, except where the Australian Consumer Law gives you a right to one.
  • Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

Pausing

  • Core and The Beast can be paused for up to 2 weeks in total during the 12 weeks.
  • Essential cannot be paused.

WhatsApp reply hours (The Beast)

For The Beast, direct WhatsApp messages get a reply within 2 hours during Australian business hours, Melbourne time.

If you are overseas, video calls are booked at a time that suits both your time zone and ours.

After your 12 weeks

  • Two weeks before your plan ends, you can renew at 20% off and keep your place.
  • Or drop to Basic for $30 every 12 weeks ($2.50 a week) and keep logging on your own. Your targets are kept.

Who we are

Beast Coach is run by Stefan. Questions about these terms: info@beastcoach.fit.
