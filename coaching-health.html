Health notice

Last updated 25 November 2026.

Stefan is a coach

Stefan is a coach. He is not a dietitian, a nutritionist or a doctor. Everything in Beast Coach is general guidance, not medical advice.

General guidance only

The meal plans, calorie and macro targets, steps targets, training plans and Beast AI estimates are general guidance for healthy adults. They are not tailored to a medical condition, and they are not a diagnosis or a treatment.

Beast AI gives estimates, not lab measurements. Check the numbers before you rely on them.

See a doctor first if

  • You have a medical condition, such as heart, blood pressure, blood sugar, kidney, thyroid or eating-related conditions.
  • You have an injury, or pain that limits how you move.
  • You take medication that could be affected by exercise or changes to what you eat or drink.

Please see a doctor before you start, and tell them about the plan.

Stop and get help straight away if you get chest pain, dizziness, fainting, or shortness of breath that does not settle during or after exercise.

Your answers matter

Tell us about any condition, injury or medication when you sign up. If you answer yes to any health question, Stefan will not activate your plan until you have confirmed that your doctor has cleared you to start.

Questions

Email info@beastcoach.fit if you are not sure whether the plan is right for you.
