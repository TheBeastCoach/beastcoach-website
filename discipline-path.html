FAQ

Is this a meal plan or workout program?

No, those are included as bonuses, but the core of the program is the 7-step discipline system. If you only want a meal plan, this isn't built for you.

How long does it take?

The Identity Reset step is designed around 30 days, but doing the whole program properly takes longer than that. Most people move through the full 7 steps over 8 to 12 weeks.

Do I need any equipment?

No. The Workout Sheet has a Home option built entirely around bodyweight, dumbbells, or bands if you don't have gym access.

Is this medical or dietary advice?

No. This reflects one person's personal experience. Please consult a doctor or accredited health professional before changing your diet or exercise routine, this is stated clearly before you begin.

What if I fall behind or miss a day?

Step 5, the Reset Protocol, exists specifically for this. You'll set your own rule for a bad day before you ever have one.

Do I get updates?

Yes, new recipes are added to the library on an ongoing basis at no extra cost.

Will my progress show up if I open this on another device?

No. Everything saves locally in your browser, on purpose, so your habit log and reflections stay private and never touch a server. That means it stays on the one device and browser you use it on, pick one and stick with it.
