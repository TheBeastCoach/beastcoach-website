How it starts

No templates. Your plan starts with you.

Every coaching plan starts the same way. What changes is how much coaching you get after that.

1

2

Is this for you?

This is for you if
  • You want a plan built for you, not a template with your name on it
  • You have tried on your own and it keeps slipping by week three
  • You will log your food and be honest in your check-ins
  • You can commit to 12 weeks
Probably not for you if
  • You want a quick fix before an event in the next few weeks
  • You are not willing to track what you eat
  • You have a diagnosed eating disorder or a medical condition that needs a doctor's guidance first

Questions.

Is this online only?

Yes. Your plan and your logging live in the members portal. Check-ins, video calls and form checks happen on WhatsApp. It works wherever you are.

Do I need a gym?

No. The questionnaire asks whether you train at home or in a gym, and your training plan is built around the equipment you have.

How accurate is Beast AI?

Close, when you give it detail. "150g chicken breast, no oil, with 150g cooked brown rice" gets a close estimate. "A bowl of pasta" gets a rough one. They are estimates, not lab measurements.

Can my coach see my food logs?

On Essential, Core and The Beast, yes. That is the point: I review what you actually logged, not what you remember. On Basic there is no coaching, so your logs are yours alone. Nobody else ever sees them.

What happens after 12 weeks?

Two weeks before you finish, you get the option to renew at 20% off and keep your place. Or drop to Basic for $2.50 AUD a week ($30 AUD, billed every 12 weeks) and keep logging and tracking on your own, with everything saved to your account.

Can I move up a plan later?

Yes. Message me on WhatsApp and I will work out the price for the weeks you have left.

What is Basic?

The entry point. For $2.50 AUD a week ($30 AUD, billed every 12 weeks) you answer a quick 2-minute setup and we work out your daily calorie, protein, carb, fat and fibre targets. Then you log your meals with Beast AI, log your steps and track your progress against those targets. No plan design, no calls and no contact with me. You can move up to a coaching plan any time.

Can I pause if I get sick?

On Core and The Beast, yes, for up to 2 weeks in total. Your end date moves so you do not lose any coaching.

What if it is not for me?

Full refund any time before your plan starts. Once your plan is built and delivered, there are no refunds.

Is this medical or dietary advice?

No. I am a coach, not a dietitian or nutritionist. Beast Coach provides general fitness and nutrition guidance. If you have a medical condition, injury or food allergy, please get clearance from your GP or a relevant health professional before starting.
