Built By Someone Who Has Been Where You Are

In 2003 I weighed close to 147kg. I lost a significant amount, but for years remained stuck at around 25% body fat. I looked okay, but just wanted to feel better. I was stuck in yoyo diets, training hard, eating reasonably well, but not moving the needle.


Six months ago I decided to make myself the priority. I learnt about my body, studied labels, tracked everything, rebuilt my training from scratch, and sought help from nutrition and exercise experts, as well as support for my mental wellbeing throughout the process.


What I found were expensive AI apps that guess calories. How does AI detect 250 calories of olive oil? I couldn't find an app precise enough, one that logged and connected my meal plan to my pantry to my shopping list, knew my day to plan my workouts and meals, and actually understood my goals and life. So I built it.

Smart Pantry

Everything Starts In Your Pantry.

The pantry is the engine of Beast Coach. Log an AI meal and the ingredients are added to your virtual pantry, and the app knows what you have or use normally. Your meal plan is built from what's actually in your kitchen. When you log a meal, the ingredients are deducted automatically. When stock runs low, it goes straight onto your shopping list.

  • Virtual pantry with full macro data per item
  • Physical pantry with low stock alerts
  • AI text, photo and barcode scanning to add items
  • Build a meal directly from pantry ingredients
  • Automatic stock deduction when meals are logged
  • Connects directly to your shopping list

Smart Shopping

Built in Shopping App That Keeps You Honest.

Your shopping list builds automatically from your meal plan and pantry stock levels, organised by aisle. Use it at the checkout to see exactly what you're putting in your body before it gets home. This has been an absolute game changer for me.

  • Auto-generated from meal plan and pantry
  • Organised by aisle: produce, meat, dairy, frozen
  • Live health score for your entire basket

  • Tick off as you shop
  • Share the list with your partner
