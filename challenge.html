30-Day Challenge

Day 1 doesn't feel like anything. That's the point.

30 days to become someone new, not with a workout plan, but with proof you can change. Notice your steps. Read a food label before you buy. Small actions, new small rules in your life repeated daily, that quietly rewrite who you think you are. Log one a day and watch it add up. Keep each one small enough to commit to. Over time, the shift changes your identity.
