Questions
Is it really free?

Yes, always free. No card needed to join, nothing to cancel. The $5 coffee inside the challenge is entirely optional and never obligated.

I've never tracked anything. Is this for me?

Yes. Start with three easy things and add more when you're ready.

Who can see my logs?

Only you. Your logs are private to your account.

What if I miss a day?

Log it the next day or the day after. If you miss longer, keep going. The challenge doesn't reset.

Do I have to finish the whole route?

No. Short routes you can finish in 30 days. The longer city to city journeys are epic, so you simply cover as much of the way as you can.
